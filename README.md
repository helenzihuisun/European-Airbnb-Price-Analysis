
# European Airbnb Pricing Analysis

An end-to-end analytics project examining what drives nightly pricing across Airbnb listings in ten European cities — from descriptive statistics and probability modeling through hypothesis testing and multiple regression.

## Dataset

- **Source:** [Kaggle — "airbnb" dataset by youssefkhaled117](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/youssefkhaled117/airbnb)
- **Topic:** Airbnb listings across ten European cities, covering price, room characteristics, host status, guest satisfaction, and location-level indices (e.g. safety, cost of nearby meals, distance to city center).
- **Size:** 51,707 listings, 12 fields, no missing values.
- **Main research question:** What listing- and location-level factors explain variation in Airbnb nightly price, and how reliable are those relationships given the data's extreme right skew and its observational (non-experimental) nature?
